Questions

Before you order.

How fresh is it, really? +
We roast Monday and post Tuesday, so it reaches most of the UK on Wednesday. That puts it two to three days off the roaster when you open it. Coffee is at its best from about day three to day twenty-one, so you are catching it properly.
Can I get it ground? +
Yes. Tell us what you brew with at checkout and we grind it that morning to match. Whole bean keeps better, so if you own a grinder we would nudge you that way, but ground is not a compromise if you are drinking it inside a fortnight.
What if I do not like it? +
Tell us within thirty days and the first bag is on us, keep it or bin it. We would rather know why, so there is a line in the email asking, but you do not have to fill it in to get your money back.
How do I pause or cancel? +
Two clicks in your account, any time before the Sunday cut-off. Skip a single week, push the whole thing back a month, or stop entirely. Nobody rings you to ask if you are sure.
Do you ship outside the UK? +
Ireland and most of the EU, posted the same Tuesday, usually two to four days in transit. Everywhere else is on the list but we are not shipping it until it arrives fresh enough to be worth the money.
